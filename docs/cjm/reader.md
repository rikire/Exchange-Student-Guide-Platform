# Reader journey

The student who needs an answer.

Agreed in phase 1, stage 1, from the stakeholder acknowledgement's four categories (administrative
procedures, campus facilities, essential services, practical life) and reconciled against the
feature list in the same sitting — see the **Feature coverage tracker** in
[01-requirements-design.md](../roadmap/01-requirements-design.md).

## Use cases

### UC-001 — Search by a known term

Arrives with a specific question and finds the answer through search.

### UC-002 — Browse without the right words

Arrives without the exact term for the question and finds the answer by browsing tags/categories.

### UC-003 — Read an article

Reads a published article in full, including any attached media.

### UC-004 — Download an attached file

Downloads a file attached to an article (for example, a PDF or a Word document).

### UC-005 — Follow a wiki link

Follows a `[[wiki link]]` from one article to a related one.

### UC-006 — Discover via backlinks

Finds related articles through "what links here" on the article they are reading.

### UC-007 — Orient from the homepage

Lands on the homepage without yet knowing what to look for, and gets oriented.

### UC-008 — Hit a red link

Follows a link that has no target yet, and understands why.

### UC-009 — Report an article

Flags an article as inaccurate, outdated or otherwise a problem, with a required message explaining
why. Routed to the moderator's inbox — see UC-019 in [moderator.md](moderator.md). Revised 10 Sep:
the message became required, not optional — see FR-021.

### UC-023 — Follow a red link to a creation prompt

Clicks a red link and is taken to a page inviting them to create the missing article. Added 6 Sep,
alongside FR-004's priority change — see the Feature coverage tracker.
