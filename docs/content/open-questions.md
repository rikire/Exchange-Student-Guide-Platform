# Open questions on the seed articles

What each seed article's author could not confirm from the sources and wants checked with OGE.
These sections were published at the end of the articles until 1 Oct (walkthrough finding F-4);
they are kept here instead, one heading per article, and are never imported. When a question is
answered, correct the article and delete the question.
