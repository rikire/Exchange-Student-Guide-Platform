Registering with FRRO

Every exchange student must register with the Foreigner Regional Registration Office (FRRO) within 14 days of arrival. Bring your passport, visa page, and admission letter to the office on Kamarajar Salai.

See also Opening a bank account — many students do both errands on the same trip since the offices are a short walk apart. If you haven't set one up yet, read SIM card registration first (this article does not exist yet).
