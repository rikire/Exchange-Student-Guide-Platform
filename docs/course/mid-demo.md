# Mid-demo

**Due Friday 9 October 2026 — 5 marks**

The scenario: a student opens the landing page, searches for "FRRO registration", reads the article,
follows a wiki link, proposes an edit with a photo of the form, and the OGE moderator approves it;
the change is live. Each step is traced to its journey, use case and screen in
[scenario-trace.md](../cjm/scenario-trace.md).

## Before the demo

- The stand runs from `docker compose up -d` and was reset on 8 October (`down -v`, then
  `up --build`): 36 seed articles, nothing submitted, the moderator's password changed — see
  [03-main-flow.md](../roadmap/03-main-flow.md), the step "The stand before the demo".
- Two browser windows: a private one for the student, a normal one for the moderator, already on
  `http://localhost:8080/moderate/login` and not yet signed in.
- A photo of Form C (or any paper form) on the laptop, under 10 MB — the stand's photo limit
  (NFR-001).

## The script

| # | Who | Do | What the audience sees | FR | Tests |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Student | Open `http://localhost:8080/` | The landing page: the search box, the pinned articles | FR-009 | `LandingControllerTest` |
| 2 | Student | Type `FRRO registration` in **Search the guide**, press **Search** | *Registering with FRRO* first in the results | FR-007 | `SearchFlowTest` |
| 3 | Student | Open *Registering with FRRO* | The article, rendered from Markdown, with its tags | FR-001 | `ArticleControllerTest` |
| 4 | Student | Click the wiki link **your student visa**, then come back | *Applying for Your Student Visa* opens; the link was written as `[[Applying for Your Student Visa\|your student visa]]` | FR-002 | `WikiLinkRendererTest` |
| 5 | Student | **Propose an edit**; add under the "Do these two forms first" list: `Form C looks like the photo below; your hostel manager has it.`; attach the photo in **Attachment (optional)**; **Submit for review** | The confirmation page with a submission number (`SUB-…`); the article itself has not changed | FR-011 | `SubmissionFlowTest` |
| 6 | Moderator | Sign in; open the queue | The submission at the top of the queue, as an edit of *Registering with FRRO* | FR-014 | `ModerationFlowTest`, `ModeratorLoginTest` |
| 7 | Moderator | **Review →** | The changed text as a diff against the published version, and the photo | FR-015 | `ModerationFlowTest` |
| 8 | Moderator | **Approve & publish**; reload the article in the student's window | The new sentence in the article and the photo under it, opening full screen on a click | FR-017, FR-032 | `ModerationFlowTest`, `BrowserPhotoViewerTest` |

What the script shows in passing: nobody needs an account to contribute (step 5), a submission is
on no public page until it is approved (steps 5 and 8), and the replaced text is kept as a revision
(FR-020).

## If something breaks

- The page does not load: `docker compose ps`; if the app is not running, `docker compose up -d`.
- The stand holds a rehearsal's edit: `docker compose down -v`, then `docker compose up --build -d`.
  This deletes every submission and upload on the stand and brings back the 36 seed articles.
