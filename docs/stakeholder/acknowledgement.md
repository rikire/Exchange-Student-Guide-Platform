## What this commits us to, and what it does not

**It does commit us to:** a centralised resource for incoming exchange students covering
administrative procedures, campus facilities, essential services and practical life at IITM and in
Chennai. That is the purpose, and it has not changed.

**It does not commit us to:** the specific feature list in the proposal. The acknowledgement calls
the MVP scope "appropriate for the project timeframe" and offers a scoping meeting; it does not
approve a fixed set of screens. Where our delivered scope differs from the proposal — a
community-editable wiki with moderation rather than a fixed set of topic pages — that difference is
stated openly in the design document rather than glossed over.

**Still outstanding:** the scoping meeting Mr. Thukaram offered has not happened. We plan it for
phase 4, when there is a working stand to react to rather than a description. That is a deliberate
trade and it carries a risk, recorded in [docs/roadmap/04-hardening.md](../roadmap/04-hardening.md):
until then, the acceptance criteria in [acceptance.md](acceptance.md) are ours, not his.
