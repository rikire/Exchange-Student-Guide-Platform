# Handover package

The Exchange Student Guide, handed over to the Office of Global Engagement, IIT Madras. Everything
needed to run it without the two students who built it.

| If you are… | Read |
|---|---|
| a moderator at OGE: you review what students send and keep the guide tidy | [moderator-guide.md](moderator-guide.md) |
| whoever looks after the server: install, HTTPS, updates, problems | [install.md](install.md) |
| either: keeping the content safe | [backup.md](backup.md) |
| anyone with a question we have not answered | [contacts.md](contacts.md) |

**What the guide is.** A website of articles for incoming exchange students. Anyone can propose a
new article or a correction, with a photo, a PDF or a video, without an account; nothing is
published until an OGE moderator approves it. Readers search, browse by tag and follow links
between articles.

**What it is not.** It has no user accounts (the moderators share one password) and no interface
for other programs; both were choices, recorded with their reasons in
[../requirements/constraints.md](../requirements/constraints.md). It sends no e-mail: a student keeps
the number a submission is given and checks its status on the site.
