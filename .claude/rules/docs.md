# Writing documentation

Full section: [docs/ai/docs-sync.md](../../docs/ai/docs-sync.md), "Writing documentation".

- Every sentence carries a fact, a decision, or a consequence of one. Cut a sentence that restates
  the heading, says why the document exists, praises its own honesty, or repeats a table above it.
- One sentence of reason per decision. An incident's story belongs in a dated record; the rule keeps
  the decision and a link.
- Sufficient, not short: do not cut a sentence that holds a decision.
- No hand-written count that a command can produce.
- Audits, the journal and `PLAN-PROMPT.md` are dated records: add a note, never rewrite.
