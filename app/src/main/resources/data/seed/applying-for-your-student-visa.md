This is Phase 3 of [[The Application Roadmap, at a Glance]], and it's the step that actually gets you
a travel document. It depends on everything before it: [[Getting Your IIT Madras Invitation Letter]],
[[Registering on the SII Portal]], and [[Filling in Your SII Student Dashboard]] all have to
be done first.

## The prerequisite: final approval from the institute

You cannot apply for a student visa the moment you register on SII. The sequence, per the SII
dashboard process, is:

1. IIT Madras (the institute) gives you a **final confirmation** after you've accepted your offer.
2. Only after that final approval are your details forwarded to the Visa module.
3. **Wait a minimum of 12 hours** after receiving final approval before applying for the visa — this
   is the portal's own explicit advice, not a guess. Applying immediately risks the visa system not
   yet reflecting your approved status.

## Where to apply

**[https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/](https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/)** — the authorised portal for
visa applications to India. The site itself warns that the Government of India has not authorised
any agent or intermediary to charge a fee for facilitating an emergency or express visa — treat any
such offer as untrustworthy.

## What you need

- Your **SII ID** (mandatory — you cannot apply for a Student Visa or E-Visa without it).
- Proof your institute fees are paid, where applicable.
- A valid passport with the validity your visa category requires.

Depending on your nationality, you may be eligible for a **Student E-Visa** through the Electronic
Travel Authorization (ETA) route rather than a regular/paper visa applied for at an Indian mission —
the portal separates these into distinct application paths.

## After the visa

Getting the visa isn't the end — once you land, [[Registering with FRRO]] has its own 14-day clock,
and you'll also need to [[Collecting Your Student ID Card|collect your student ID card]] within the
window IIT Madras sets.

- Typical visa processing time once the SII ID and final approval are in place, so students can plan
  travel dates realistically.
