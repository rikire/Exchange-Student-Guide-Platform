This is Phase 1 of [[The Application Roadmap, at a Glance]], and nothing else in the process starts
without it.

## Step 1 — Your home university nominates you

IIT Madras does not accept direct applications for the exchange programme from individual students.
The nomination has to come from your home institution officially. If you haven't been nominated yet,
none of the steps below are available to you — go back to your home university's exchange/study-abroad
office first, and check [[Exchange Programme Eligibility]] for what they'll be confirming when they
nominate you.

## Step 2 — IIT Madras issues your letters

Once the nomination is in, IIT Madras issues three documents together:

- Your **student invitation letter**
- An **incorporation letter**
- An **undertaking letter**

## What these letters are for

These aren't just for your records. The invitation letter specifically is what an IIT Madras liaison
later uploads into the SII portal — that's step 3 of the Visa Flow described in
[[Registering on the SII Portal]] — and it's what you'll be asked to show when you
[[Collecting Your Student ID Card|collect your student ID card]] on arrival. Keep all three letters,
in both digital and printed form, for the rest of the process.

## What comes next

With the invitation letter issued, you move to Phase 3 of the roadmap — registering on the SII portal
and applying for your visa. Course selection (Phase 2, [[Selecting Courses on the SSP Portal]]) can
happen in parallel; it isn't blocked by the letters.
