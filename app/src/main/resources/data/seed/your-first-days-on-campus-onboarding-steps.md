Arriving on campus starts a short chain of formalities. Most of them are immigration paperwork that
Indian law requires for every foreign student. Here is the order OGE's
[International Student Handbook 2026](https://ge.iitm.ac.in/assets/pdf/international-student-support-handbook.pdf?version=v2)
(page 4) lays out.

## The steps

1. **Reach your hostel or guest house.** If you arrive outside office hours, see
   [[Getting from Chennai Airport to IIT Madras]].
2. **Form C.** The hostel or guest house completes it for you. It records where a foreign national
   is staying. Complete it with your hostel manager once you have settled in.
3. **Visit the Office of Global Engagement (OGE)** at the time OGE gives you.
4. **Meet the International Student Advisor.**
5. **Form S** and **registration in IIT Madras's systems**, both done with OGE.
6. **Register on the e-FRRO portal within 14 days of arrival.** This applies if your visa carries
   the endorsement that requires registration. The details are in [[Registering with FRRO]].
7. **Receive your e-FRRO certificate.** OGE fixes a meeting for this.

The handbook stresses that Form C (with your hostel manager) and Form S (with OGE) are
**mandatory**.

## Also in your first week

- **Collect your student ID card.** You need it to get onto campus and to identify yourself. See
  [[Collecting Your Student ID Card]].
- **Pay your semester and hostel fees** on the fee portal and download the e-receipt. See
  [[Paying Your Institute Fees]].
- **Get online.** See [[Connecting to the Campus Wi-Fi]].
- **Save the numbers** in [[Emergency and Important Contacts]] before you need them.

## The 14-day deadline is real

If you miss the FRRO deadline, you pay **penalty charges** and have to write a letter explaining the
delay (handbook, page 5). The clock starts when you land in India, not when you reach campus. If you
travel in India first, you have less time than you think.
