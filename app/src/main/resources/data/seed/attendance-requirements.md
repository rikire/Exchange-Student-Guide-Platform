Attendance at IIT Madras is graded on its own scale, separate from
[[How Grading Works at IIT Madras|your course grade]]. OGE's brochure gives the exact bands:

| Attendance | Category | Code |
|---|---|---|
| ≥ 95% | Very Good | VG |
| 80–94% | Good | G |
| 75–79% | Marginal | M |
| < 75% | Poor | P |

## Where the real risk starts

The jump from "Marginal" to "Poor" happens at **75%** — miss more than a quarter of a course's
sessions and you're in the lowest band. Treat 75% as a hard floor to stay above, not a target: the
"Marginal" band itself (75–79%) is already a warning zone, not a safe one.

## Why this matters more for an exchange student

A single semester gives you less room to recover from a bad early stretch — a rough first few weeks
adjusting to [[Understanding the IIT Madras Credit System|the workload]] or the city can put a dent in
attendance that's hard to claw back before the term ends. Track it yourself from week one rather than
finding out your percentage late in the semester.
