Once you're inside [[Selecting Courses on the SSP Portal]], your level of study changes what happens
to your requests.

## Undergraduate guidelines

UG students must select UG-level courses appropriate to their current level of study. You can't reach
into postgraduate-level courses just because a topic interests you — the level has to match where you
actually are in your programme at your home institution.

## Postgraduate guidelines

PG-level course requests are **strictly subject to faculty approval.** This is a stronger gate than
the general review every request goes through — expect closer scrutiny of prerequisites and fit.

## The review mechanism, either way

All courses — UG or PG — are thoroughly reviewed by IIT Madras instructors. Every request lands in
one of three outcomes:

- **Approve the request** — you're in.
- **Suggest an alternate course** — the instructor thinks something else fits better; you decide
  whether to accept the suggestion.
- **Reject the request** — most common when level or prerequisites don't line up.

## What this means practically

Build your course list from [[Choosing a Department or School]] with your actual academic level in
mind from the start, rather than picking ambitiously and hoping review sorts it out — a PG student
requesting an unusual mix, or a UG student reaching too high, adds a round of back-and-forth that
eats into the time [[Selecting Courses on the SSP Portal|the SSP window]] gives you.
