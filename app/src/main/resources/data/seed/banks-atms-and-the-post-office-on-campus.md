You don't have to leave campus for cash: there are **two banks and several ATMs** inside. Locations
are from OGE's
[International Student Handbook 2026](https://ge.iitm.ac.in/assets/pdf/international-student-support-handbook.pdf?version=v2),
page 11. The directions use [[Campus Landmarks You Need to Know|campus landmarks]], mostly Gajendra
Circle.

## Banks

| Bank | Where |
|---|---|
| **State Bank of India (SBI)** | On Bonn Avenue, opposite the Kendriya Vidyalaya (KV) school and the post office, about 200 m from Gajendra Circle |
| **Canara Bank** | Campus Shopping Centre, on Delhi Avenue |

## ATMs

- **SBI**, opposite Kendriya Vidyalaya
- **SBI**, at Taramani Guest House (towards the hostel zone)
- **Canara Bank**, at the Campus Shopping Centre
- **Canara Bank**, at the Student Facilities Centre

The handbook also lists ATM locations **near the Office of Hostel Management** and **near Nilgiri**,
without saying which bank they belong to.

## Post office

The **campus post office** is on Bonn Avenue near the KV school, opposite SBI. A **railway ticket
booking counter** with limited hours shares the building. It is useful for booking Indian Railways
trips in person.

## Paying fees

Institute and hostel fees are paid online on the fee portal, not at these branches. See
[[Paying Your Institute Fees]].
