Chennai's malls are where the city goes to shop, eat, watch films and, in the hot months, simply
enjoy the air-conditioning. They are also the easiest place to find things you need in your first
weeks: a SIM card, bedding, a pressure cooker, a laptop charger, or familiar international brands.

Most malls have a supermarket, a food court with dishes from all over India and beyond, and a
multiplex cinema. Films are shown in Tamil, Hindi, English and other languages; the listing says
which, and a Tamil blockbuster on its opening weekend is an experience in its own right.

## Phoenix Marketcity, Velachery — the closest to campus

**Phoenix Marketcity** opened in 2013 in Velachery, a short ride from the campus, which makes it
the mall IIT Madras students know best. Next to it, in the same compound, is **Palladium**, with
more upmarket brands.

Good for: everyday shopping, electronics, a large food court, the cinema, and a first outing with
new friends.

## Express Avenue, Royapettah — in the city centre

**Express Avenue** opened in 2010 on Whites Road in Royapettah, in the middle of the city. It is a
good base for a day in central Chennai: the Marina and the old buildings around Chennai Central are
a short ride away.

Good for: combining shopping with sightseeing, a large gaming arcade, and a meal after an afternoon
at the beach.

![The entrance of Express Avenue mall](/img/articles/malls/express-avenue.jpg)
*Express Avenue on Whites Road.*

## VR Chennai, Anna Nagar — for an evening out

**VR Chennai** opened in 2018 in Anna Nagar, in the west of the city. Its facade, lit in coloured
squares after dark, is one of the city's newer landmarks, and it has more than 200 stores along
with restaurants and a cinema.

Good for: an evening out, a long weekend lunch, and a look at one of Chennai's liveliest residential
neighbourhoods.

![The facade of VR Chennai lit in coloured squares at dusk](/img/articles/malls/vr-chennai.jpg)
*VR Chennai's facade at dusk, Anna Nagar.*

## Tips for a mall day

- Weekday afternoons are calm; weekend evenings are lively and busy.
- Bags are usually checked at the entrance. It is routine and quick.
- Ride-hailing apps have marked pick-up points at the bigger malls; see
  [[Getting Around Campus and Chennai]].
- Cards and UPI (India's instant mobile payments) are accepted almost everywhere; see
  [[Banks, ATMs and the Post Office on Campus]].

If you have had enough of air-conditioning, the sea is waiting: [[Chennai's Beaches]].
