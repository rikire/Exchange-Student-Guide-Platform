Study in India (SII) is the Government of India's national portal for foreign students — run by the
Ministry of Education through the implementing agency EdCIL (India) Limited, and used by over 900
Indian institutes, not just IIT Madras. If you've been nominated for the IIT Madras exchange
programme, you'll use this same national system, but with one important difference from a student
applying cold: IIT Madras's liaison handles the institute side of your application directly, because
you already have an [[Getting Your IIT Madras Invitation Letter|invitation letter]].

## How the two flows fit together

OGE's own diagram splits this into two connected flows:

**Invitation flow (already done by this point):**
1. Your home university officially nominates you.
2. IIT Madras issues your invitation letter, incorporation letter, and undertaking letter.

**Visa flow (this article, requires the SII portal):**
1. Log in to the SII portal and fill in the required personal and academic information.
2. Select **"IIT Madras"** as your institution and **"Semester Exchange"** as the course/programme.
3. The IIT Madras liaison accepts your application and uploads your invitation letter.
4. You receive your **SII ID** — mandatory for your final visa application.

## Registering, step by step

1. Go to [https://studyinindia.gov.in/](https://studyinindia.gov.in/) and choose **Register → Student
   Registration**.
2. Fill in the pop-up form: email address, password, first and last name, country/nationality,
   country code and mobile number, and the CAPTCHA shown.
3. Submit. A one-time password (OTP) is emailed to the address you gave.
4. Enter the OTP in the verification pop-up and click **Verify OTP** — this completes registration.
5. Log in with **Log in → Student Login**, using your email, password, and a fresh CAPTCHA.

## What your SII ID looks like

The ID follows a fixed pattern: **Year – Country ISD Code – Unique Identifier Code.** For example, a
student registering in 2026 whose country's calling code is 975 would see an ID starting `26-975-`,
followed by a system-assigned identifier number. Your own ID will carry your own country's calling
code, not necessarily that one — the digits after it are simply assigned by the portal, not something
to predict.
