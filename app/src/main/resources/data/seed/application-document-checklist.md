Several other articles in this guide each ask for documents at different points in the process. This
one exists purely to consolidate them into a single checklist you can work through before you start,
rather than discovering a missing document mid-application.

## For IIT Madras directly (see [[Uploading Your SII Documents]])

- Home Institute ID Card (PDF, ≤ 1.5 MB)
- Curriculum Vitae / CV (PDF, ≤ 1.5 MB)
- English Proficiency Certificate (PDF, ≤ 1.5 MB)
- Nomination Letter from Current Institute (PDF, ≤ 1.5 MB)
- Latest Transcript (PDF, ≤ 1.5 MB)
- Student Signature (JPG/JPEG/PNG, ≤ 2 MB)
- Student Photo, for ID card purposes (JPG/JPEG/PNG, ≤ 2 MB)

## For the SII portal (see [[Filling in Your SII Student Dashboard]])

- Passport / national ID proof (PDF/JPG/PNG, ≤ 2 MB)
- Medical fitness certificate (PDF/JPG/PNG, ≤ 2 MB)
- Transcripts/marksheets for each academic qualification you report (PDF only, ≤ 2 MB)
- Passport details: full name as per passport, passport number, issuing authority, place and country
  of issue, dates of issue and expiry

## On arrival (see [[Collecting Your Student ID Card]] and [[Registering with FRRO]])

- A printed or clearly legible copy of your IIT Madras invitation letter
- Your passport and visa, for FRRO registration

## How to use this

Gather everything above before [[Registering on the SII Portal|you start the SII registration]] —
scanning and compressing documents under deadline pressure is where most avoidable delays happen.
Keep both a full-resolution original and a size-compressed copy of every scan, since the same
document often needs to go into more than one system with different size limits.
