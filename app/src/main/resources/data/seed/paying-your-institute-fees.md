Once you've joined the Institute, there's one more administrative step before you're fully settled:
paying your fees through the dedicated portal.

## What OGE's brochure says

Students must visit the dedicated fee portal to pay applicable semester and hostel fees **after
joining the Institute** — this is not something done before arrival, unlike most of the rest of the
application process. Once payment is completed, **students must download the e-receipt directly from
the portal.**

## Don't skip the receipt

The instruction to download the e-receipt is specific and deliberate — treat it as a required step,
not an optional courtesy copy. Keep it alongside the documents in [[Application Document Checklist]];
a fee dispute or a request for proof of payment later is much easier to resolve with the portal's own
receipt in hand than by trying to recall a bank transaction.

## What this covers

"Applicable semester and hostel fees" ties this directly to [[Hostel and Mess Accommodation]] — if
you've been allotted a hostel room, its fee is paid through this same portal alongside your semester
fee, not separately.

## Needs checking with OGE before this goes in front of students

- The actual fee amounts for semester and hostel charges — none are published in the source material
  this guide was written from, and a specific number should come from OGE directly rather than be
  estimated here.
- The exact URL or name of the fee portal, and whether it's the same login as the SII or SSP systems
  or a separate IIT Madras–only system.
- Accepted payment methods (international card, wire transfer, etc.) for students paying from abroad.
