If you're reading this because you just landed in Chennai: the clock is already running. You have
to register with the Foreigners Regional Registration Office (FRRO) **within 14 days of arriving in
India**. This comes after [[Applying for Your Student Visa|your student visa]] and belongs to your
first days on campus; see [[Your First Days on Campus: Onboarding Steps]] for where it fits.

The requirements below are from OGE's
[International Student Handbook 2026](https://ge.iitm.ac.in/assets/pdf/international-student-support-handbook.pdf?version=v2),
pages 4–5.

## You don't do this alone

Start by contacting the **OGE Admissions team**. They guide and help international students through
the FRRO procedure. The Study in India material says the same: your institute helps you get the
documents and details the registration needs.

## Do these two forms first

Once you have settled in your hostel, two forms are **mandatory**:

- **Form C**, completed with your **hostel manager** (or at the guest house).
- **Form S**, completed with the **Office of Global Engagement (OGE)**.

## Documents you need (UG and PG students)

| Document | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| Photograph | you |
| Passport | you |
| Visa | you |
| Offer letter / admission letter from IIT Madras | IIT Madras |
| Sponsorship letter, as a PDF (ICCR, IIT Madras scholarship, SII, etc.) | your sponsor |
| Bonafide certificate | OGE |
| Letter of undertaking | OGE |
| Form C | your hostel or guest house |

PhD students also need a synopsis of their project and **Form G** (issued by OGE).

## It's done online, not in person

The registration itself is online, on the e-FRRO portal:

**[https://indianfrro.gov.in/eservices/](https://indianfrro.gov.in/eservices/)**

You do not need to visit an office, and you do not need an agent. The portal's own advisory is
explicit: no intermediary or agent is authorised to charge a fee for "speedy" or "express" FRRO
registration, and anyone offering that is not legitimate.

1. On the portal, choose **"New User? Register here"**.
2. Fill in your personal and passport details, exactly as on your
   [[Applying for Your Student Visa|visa]].
3. Upload the documents in the table above.
4. Submit, and keep the application number you are given.
5. When the registration is approved, you receive an **e-FRRO certificate**. OGE sets up a meeting
   for this step.

## If you miss the 14 days

Registering late costs you **penalty charges**, and you have to give a **letter explaining the
reason for the delay**. The 14 days count from the day you land in India, not from the day you reach
campus. If you travel first, start early.

## Needs checking with OGE before this goes in front of students

  student gives as the "offer letter / admission letter" and the "sponsorship letter" if they have
  no sponsor.
- Typical processing time at the Chennai FRRO.
- Whether a short exchange changes the requirement. Some Indian sources tie compulsory registration
  to visas longer than 180 days, and the handbook doesn't mention this.
- The Study in India slides give the deadline once as 14 days and once as 15. The handbook says 14,
  and this article follows the handbook.
