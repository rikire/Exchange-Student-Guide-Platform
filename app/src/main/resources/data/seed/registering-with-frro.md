If you're reading this because you just landed in Chennai: the clock is already running. Registering
with the Foreigner Regional Registration Office (FRRO) is mandatory for every international student,
and it has to happen **within 14 days of arrival** — after registering for
[[Applying for Your Student Visa|your student visa]] and before you settle into campus life
properly.

## It's done online, not in person

The process is called e-FRRO, and it runs entirely through the government portal:

**[https://indianfrro.gov.in/eservices/](https://indianfrro.gov.in/eservices/)**

You do not need to visit an office, and you do not need an agent. The portal's own advisory is
explicit about this — no intermediary or agent is authorised to charge a fee for "speedy" or
"express" FRRO registration, and anyone offering that is not legitimate.

## Steps

1. Go to the e-FRRO portal and choose **"New User? Register here"**.
2. Fill in your personal and passport details — the same details your
   [[Applying for Your Student Visa|visa]] was issued against.
3. Upload the documents the portal asks for (a recent photo, your passport bio page, your visa page).
4. Submit the application. You'll get a reference/application number — keep it.
5. Wait for confirmation. Processing time varies; this is one of the facts below that needs
   confirming with OGE before you rely on a specific number of days.

## What IIT Madras helps with

The brochure OGE distributes says plainly: **your institute will help you get the necessary
documents and details for e-FRRO registration.** In practice this usually means the
[[Collecting Your Student ID Card|Academic Courses Section]] or your department's
international-students contact can tell you exactly which supporting letter from IIT Madras the
portal will ask for. Don't guess — ask before you submit.

## Needs checking with OGE before this goes in front of students

- The exact supporting document IIT Madras issues for e-FRRO (a letter? a stamped form?) and where to
  request it.
- Typical processing time for the Chennai FRRO office specifically.
- What happens if the 14-day window is missed because of a delayed visa or late arrival — is there a
  grace process, or a penalty?

These are the kind of details a wrong guess on gets someone in real trouble with immigration
authorities, so none of them are invented here.
