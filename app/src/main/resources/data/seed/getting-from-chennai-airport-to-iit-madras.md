You land at Chennai International Airport (the handbook calls it Anna International Airport). IIT
Madras is about **12 km** away, on Sardar Patel Road, midway between Raj Bhavan and Adyar. Everything
below comes from OGE's
[International Student Handbook 2026](https://ge.iitm.ac.in/assets/pdf/international-student-support-handbook.pdf?version=v2),
pages 2–3.

## Time your arrival

Plan your flight so you reach campus **Monday to Friday, between 10:00 am and 6:00 pm**. Those are
the Office of Global Engagement's (OGE) working hours. The office is closed at weekends and on
institute holidays.

- **Within office hours:** go to OGE and meet the Admissions team, then the International Student
  Advisor during their office hours. What happens next is in
  [[Your First Days on Campus: Onboarding Steps]].
- **Outside office hours:** report to the Office of Hostel Management (CCW) or to the hostel you were
  assigned.

OGE is on the second floor of the Sudha & Shankar Innovation Hub, inside the campus.

## Getting a cab

If you have luggage, a taxi is the easiest option:

- **Prepaid Taxi or Call Taxi.** The handbook puts the fare at about **₹600**.
- **Ride-hailing apps:** Ola, Uber and Namma Yatri all work from the airport. Install them before
  you fly, from the App Store or Google Play. The handbook shows fares between roughly **₹400 and
  ₹600** for this trip.

## Finding your way in

Useful landmarks: **Guindy National Park** is next to the IITM main gate, and the **Central Leather
Research Institute (CLRI)** campus is opposite it.

At the **IN gate** there is a security check post. From there, two roads, Delhi Avenue and Bonn
Avenue, lead to **Gajendra Circle**, next to the Administration Block and the Central Library. The
academic zone and the hostels are **3.8 km** inside the main gate. That is too far to walk with
luggage, so ask the cab to take you all the way in. The campus bus is described in
[[Getting Around Campus and Chennai]].

For where things are once you are inside, see [[Campus Landmarks You Need to Know]].
