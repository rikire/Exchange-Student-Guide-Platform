The campus is large: the hostels and the academic zone are 3.8 km from the main gate. So you will
use the campus bus every day, and the city's transport whenever you leave. All of this comes from
OGE's
[International Student Handbook 2026](https://ge.iitm.ac.in/assets/pdf/international-student-support-handbook.pdf?version=v2),
page 3.

## Inside the campus: the campus bus

- Runs **every day, from 6:15 am to 9:30 pm, every 15 minutes.**
- The route goes between the **Main Gate and the hostel zone**, in both directions. The first buses
  leave at 6:15 am from each end.
- The **last bus** leaves the Main Gate at **10:00 pm** and goes to the Bus Depot via Gajendra
  Circle.

Gajendra Circle is the main junction everyone gives directions from. See
[[Campus Landmarks You Need to Know]].

## Leaving campus

| Mode | Nearest point | Distance |
|---|---|---|
| City bus (MTC) | Cancer Institute, CLRI, Madhya Kailash stops | near the Main Gate |
| City bus (MTC) | Gandhi Road, Guru Nanak College stops | near the Velachery Gate |
| Suburban train (MRTS) | Kasturba Nagar station, near Madhya Kailash | 1.2 km from the Main Gate |
| Metro | Little Mount station | about 3 km |
| Metro | Guindy station | about 3.3 km |

The metro stations are **5–10 minutes by road**. Buses to other cities leave from the **Chennai
Mofussil Bus Terminus (CMBT)** in Koyambedu.

Route and timetable information:

- City buses: [mtcbus.tn.gov.in](https://mtcbus.tn.gov.in)
- Metro: [chennaimetrorail.org](https://chennaimetrorail.org)

## Autos and cabs

Auto-rickshaws and taxis are everywhere in Chennai. The apps **Ola, Uber and Namma Yatri** all work.
They are also how most students get from the airport, see
[[Getting from Chennai Airport to IIT Madras]].
