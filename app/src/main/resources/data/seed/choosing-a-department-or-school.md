Before you go anywhere near [[Selecting Courses on the SSP Portal]], it helps to know the actual
range of departments and schools IIT Madras teaches across. OGE's brochure lists the academic
landscape as follows.

## Engineering and core science departments

- Aerospace Engineering
- Applied Mechanics & Biomedical Engineering
- Bio Technology
- Chemical Engineering
- Civil Engineering
- Computer Science & Engineering
- Data Science and AI
- Engineering Design
- Electrical Engineering
- Mechanical Engineering
- Medical Science and Technology
- Ocean Engineering
- Physics
- Chemistry
- Mathematics

## Schools and interdisciplinary programmes

- Humanities & Social Sciences
- Management Studies
- School of Interdisciplinary Studies

## Global language courses

Offered alongside the departments above, not tied to any one of them: German, French, Korean,
Chinese, Japanese. See [[Global Language Courses]] for more on taking one of these alongside your
main coursework.

## How to use this list

This is a starting point for building your course wishlist before the SSP window opens in June —
it is not a guarantee that every listed department accepts exchange students into every course. Cross
-check specifics against [[Course Selection Rules for UG and PG Students]] once you know your level,
and against [[Understanding the IIT Madras Credit System]] once you're estimating how many courses you
can actually carry.

## Needs checking with OGE before this goes in front of students

- Whether every department and school listed here is open to semester-exchange students specifically,
  or whether some are outgoing-only or research-only.
