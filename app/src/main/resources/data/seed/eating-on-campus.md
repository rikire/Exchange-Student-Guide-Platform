You will eat most meals at your **mess**. When you want a change, there is a surprising number of
cafés and food joints on campus. Locations are from OGE's
[International Student Handbook 2026](https://ge.iitm.ac.in/assets/pdf/international-student-support-handbook.pdf?version=v2),
pages 10, 13–14 and 20.

## The mess

Most messes are in the **Himalaya, Nilgiri and Vindhya dining facility**, next to Gurunath. The mess
is **paid**, like the hostel, see [[Hostel and Mess Accommodation]]. Mess and hostel matters run
through the hostel portal **i-kollege**
([ikollege.iitm.ac.in](https://ikollege.iitm.ac.in/iitmhostel/)). For mess biometric or i-kollege
problems, call extension **8509** (044-2257-8509).

## Food courts, cafés and snacks

| Place | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Food for Thoughts (FFT) | food court | first floor of Campus Cafe |
| Isthara Food Court | food court | HFC |
| Zaitoon Restaurant & Grill | restaurant | Quark |
| Café Coffee Day (CCD) | café | near DoMS |
| Usha Café | snacks | Shop No. 01, NSFC |
| Rotorman's | food truck | behind Taramani Guest House (TGH) |
| Thouway Technologies (Cookincloud) | food | Hangout (A), ICICI Bank |

Quark, where Zaitoon is, is the student activity space near Saraswathi hostel. Its restaurant is on
the ground floor.

The handbook's own checklist for newcomers suggests trying **Zaitoon, Campus Cafe and Suprabha** so
you don't only eat at the mess.

## Groceries and everyday things

**Prime Mart**, a general store, is near the CCW office. More shops are in the Campus Shopping
Centre on Delhi Avenue, next to Canara Bank. See [[Banks, ATMs and the Post Office on Campus]].

For finding these places, see [[Campus Landmarks You Need to Know]].
