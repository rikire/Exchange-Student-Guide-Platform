Chennai lies along the Bay of Bengal, and its beaches are where the whole city comes to breathe in
the evening. Families walk by the water, children fly kites, vendors sell roasted corn and ice
cream, and the sea breeze arrives just as the day's heat begins to fade. Early morning is lovely
too: fishing boats come in, and the beach belongs to joggers and people doing yoga.

## Besant Nagar (Elliot's Beach) — the students' favourite

**Elliot's Beach**, known to everyone as **Besant Nagar beach** or "Bessie", is the closest of the
city beaches to campus and the one students go to most. It is cleaner and calmer than the Marina,
and the road behind it is lined with cafés, snack stalls and ice-cream shops, which makes it the
easy answer to "where shall we go this evening?"

Nearby are the **Ashtalakshmi Temple**, right by the sea, and **Velankanni Church**, both lovely
to visit.

## Marina Beach — the city's great promenade

The **Marina** runs for about six kilometres from near Fort St George southwards, and is often
called the second-longest urban beach in the world. Its sand is wide enough to feel like a small
desert at midday, and in the evening it fills with families, food stalls and small rides. The road
beside it passes many of the city's old buildings and memorials, which makes it a good stretch for
a walk or a slow drive.

## Along the East Coast Road

South of the city, the **East Coast Road** (ECR) runs beside the sea past quieter beaches, fishing
villages and seafront restaurants. **Covelong** (Kovalam) is known for its wide, peaceful beach and
for surfing, and the ride along the ECR is part of the pleasure. Keep going, and you reach
[[A Day Trip to Mahabalipuram]].

![Clouds over a wide, empty Covelong beach](/img/articles/beaches/covelong-beach.jpg)
*The wide sands of Covelong, on the East Coast Road.*

## Enjoy the water from the shore

The sea off Chennai has strong currents, and **swimming and bathing at the Marina are not allowed
by law**. At the city beaches, the lovely way to enjoy the water is to paddle at the edge, feel the
waves on your feet, and leave swimming for a pool. If you would like to go into the sea, a surf
school with instructors on the ECR is the right place to do it.

## Good to know

- The best time is from late afternoon until after sunset, when the sand has cooled.
- Bring a little cash for snacks. A cone of sundal (spiced chickpeas) or a plate of hot bajji
  (fritters) by the sea is a Chennai ritual.
- To get there and back: [[Getting Around Campus and Chennai]].
