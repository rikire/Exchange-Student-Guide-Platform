For everyday prayers and Friday prayer you do not need to leave campus: see
[[Muslim Prayer Hall on Campus]]. A mosque outside is useful for **Eid prayers**, for Friday when
you are already in the city, or if you simply want a larger congregation.

> **Check before you go.** The addresses below come from public directories, not from the mosques
> or OGE. Friday prayer times differ from mosque to mosque and change with the season. Confirm the
> time with MSA or a local before you travel.

## When you visit

Dress modestly, take your shoes off at the entrance, and ask before taking photos. Women should
check whether the mosque has a sisters' section before going. More on everyday customs:
[[India in Your First Weeks: Greetings, Food and Everyday Customs]].
