## Ragging is prohibited

"Ragging" is the Indian term for seniors harassing or humiliating new students. At IIT Madras it is
**strictly prohibited**. A senior who starts a friendly chat, shares tips or lends you books is
normal. Anything that makes you uncomfortable is not.

If it happens, contact **your Saathi student mentor** or **your hostel warden** straight away. The
wardens' numbers are in [[Emergency and Important Contacts]].

## Sexual harassment: zero tolerance

IIT Madras has a **zero-tolerance policy** towards sexual harassment. Under the law, this includes
physical contact, sexual advances, requests for sexual favours, sexually coloured remarks, showing
pornography, and any other unwelcome conduct of a sexual nature.

Complaints are investigated by the **Complaint Committee Against Sexual Harassment (CCASH)**, also
called the Internal Complaint Committee. It follows the guidelines of the Supreme Court of India.

- **Contact the Chairperson:** ccash@iitm.ac.in
- You can also report a grievance to the **Head of your Department**.
- More information: [CCASH page](https://www.iitm.ac.in/the-institute/administration/complaint-committee)

When harassment is confirmed, disciplinary action follows, whoever did it: student, staff or
faculty. Serious offences go to the police for prosecution under Indian law.

- For stress, anxiety or anything that feels too much, the Wellness Centre and a **24/7 helpline**
  are in [[Health Care and Wellness Support]].

## Other support services

The handbook also lists [Saathi](https://www.saathi.iitm.ac.in), [Mitr](https://www.mitr.iitm.ac.in)
and [Behappy](https://behappy.iitm.ac.in) as student support services, and the **Office of Global
Engagement** is there for any international student with a problem, academic or not.
