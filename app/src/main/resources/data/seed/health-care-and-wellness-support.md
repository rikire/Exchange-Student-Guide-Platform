Before you need them, know two places on campus: the **Institute Hospital** for physical health and
the **Wellness Centre** for mental health. Unless noted otherwise, facts are from OGE's
[International Student Handbook 2026](https://ge.iitm.ac.in/assets/pdf/international-student-support-handbook.pdf?version=v2),
pages 10, 14 and 26.

## Medical insurance is compulsory

IIT Madras's 2026 fee circular says it plainly: **"Foreign Nationals must have valid medical
insurance coverage for the duration of their stay at IIT Madras."**
([Fees circular for new students, 2026](https://fees.iitm.ac.in/assets/circular/fees_structure_2026_admission_ug_pg.pdf),
note 4.) Arrange insurance that covers you in India for your **whole** exchange before you travel.
Keep a copy of the policy on your phone.

## Institute Hospital

- Provides **primary health care** and is on **First Cross Road**.
- **Hospital enquiries:** 044-2257-**8333** or 044-2257-**8888**
- **Ambulance:** 044-2257-**8888** or 044-2257-**8333**

Every emergency number is in [[Emergency and Important Contacts]].

## Wellness Centre: counselling and mental health

The Wellness Centre offers **counselling and psychotherapy**. It is on the **first floor of the Dean
of Students (DoST) building**. Two **visiting psychiatrists** also see students at the Institute
Hospital; book them through a hospital appointment.

| Helpline | Number | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| Wellness Centre | 044 2257 8521 | 9 am – 5 pm |
| YourDOST | 044 4813 6222 | **24/7** |

Website: [wellnessiitm.com](https://www.wellnessiitm.com/)

Moving to a new country for a semester is hard, even when everything goes well. You don't need a
crisis to talk to someone.
