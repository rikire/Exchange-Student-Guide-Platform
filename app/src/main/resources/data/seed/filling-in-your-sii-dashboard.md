Once you're logged in after [[Registering on the SII Portal]], the dashboard shows your Application
Journey (Registered → Profile → Apply → Admission) and two sections you have to complete before you
can do anything else: **Basic Information** and **Academic Information**.

## Basic Information

Click **View** on the Basic Information card. It's a six-step form:

1. **Basic Information** — name, gender, date of birth, email, origin country, country code, contact
   number, marital status.
2. **Parents Information** — father's name, mother's name, an emergency contact number.
3. **Address** — country, state/province, city/town, ZIP/postal code, full residential address.
4. **Passport Information** — full name as per passport, passport number, issuing authority, place
   and country of issue, dates of issue and expiry. **Mandatory for every foreign student except
   those from Nepal and Bhutan.**
5. **Documents** — upload your passport (or national ID) and a medical fitness certificate, PDF/JPG/
   PNG, max 2 MB each. You then tick "I confirm this document is authentic" for each. This step is
   not mandatory, but strongly recommended — don't skip it and hope to fix it later.
6. **Citizenship** — students from Nepal and Bhutan must fill in their national citizenship number
   here (mandatory for them specifically). Click **Confirm & Complete Registration** to finish this
   section; you're then redirected automatically into Academic Information.

## Academic Information

For each qualification level that applies to you (secondary school, higher secondary, diploma,
graduation, post-graduation, M.Phil., other examinations like GATE/GMAT/GRE/JEE/SAT/TOEFL), toggle it
on, enter your marks or percentage/CGPA, and upload the matching transcript or marksheet (PDF only,
max 2 MB). Click **Save Academic Profile** when done — you're redirected to **Explore Courses**.

## Why this matters for the visa

None of this is optional busywork: your SII ID and this profile are what get checked when you later
[[Applying for Your Student Visa|apply for your student visa]], and the portal says explicitly that
your application "will be verified during issuance of your Student Visa" — so incomplete or
inconsistent details here can hold up your visa later, not just the SII step itself.
