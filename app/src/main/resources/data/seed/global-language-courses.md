Alongside the engineering departments and schools in [[Choosing a Department or School]], IIT Madras
also runs global language courses. The five on offer, per OGE's own materials, are:

- German
- French
- Korean
- Chinese
- Japanese

## Where this fits your schedule

These are picked the same way as any other course — through [[Selecting Courses on the SSP Portal]],
subject to the same instructor review described in [[Course Selection Rules for UG and PG Students]].
They count toward your overall credit load the same as any other course, so weigh one against
[[Understanding the IIT Madras Credit System]]'s workload cap before adding it on top of a full
technical schedule.

## Why this might matter for an exchange student specifically

A semester exchange is a short window to try something your home institution may not offer at all,
or to keep a language you've already started from lapsing. Note also
[[Exchange Programme Eligibility]]'s language-proficiency requirement applies to the *programme
you're admitted under*, not
to these elective language courses — taking Japanese as an elective doesn't require you to already
know Japanese.
