This is Phase 2 of [[The Application Roadmap, at a Glance]] — choosing and locking in what you'll
actually study. It runs on a separate system from SII: the **SSP portal**.

## When it opens

OGE's brochure marks this plainly: **the process opens in June.** If you're checking earlier than
that expecting to select courses, you're too early — the portal isn't the bottleneck, the calendar is.

## The five steps

1. **Review the courses offered** by departments and the official course list, before you log in
   anywhere.
2. **Log into the SSP portal.**
3. **Select your preferred courses** in the system.
4. **Course instructors review your request** — they may accept it, reject it, or suggest an
   alternative course. This is a real academic review, not an automatic approval.
5. **The final list of selected courses is finalized** once every request has been through step 4.

## Rules that shape what you can pick

Not every course is open to every student — see [[Course Selection Rules for UG and PG Students]]
for the level-appropriate rules, and [[Choosing a Department or School]] for the actual range of
departments and schools you're choosing from.

## Plan for rejection or substitution

Because step 4 is a genuine review, don't build a schedule with zero flexibility. If an instructor
suggests an alternative, having already looked at [[Choosing a Department or School]] and
[[Global Language Courses]] means you're not choosing a replacement course blind, under time
pressure.

## How this fits your credit load

Once your course list is finalized, check it against [[Understanding the IIT Madras Credit System]]
— the workload cap (66 credit hours a semester) is enforced regardless of how many courses instructors
approved.
