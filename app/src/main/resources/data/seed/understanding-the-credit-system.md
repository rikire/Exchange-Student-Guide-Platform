Before you finalize anything in [[Selecting Courses on the SSP Portal]], understand what a "credit"
actually costs you in time.

## The basic unit

**1 credit = 50 minutes (one credit hour) of lecture per week.**

## What that 50 minutes actually implies

A 1-credit-hour lecture requires **2 credit hours of outside classroom work** on top of it —
homework and tutorials. So a course that looks like "3 credits" on paper is really 3 hours of lecture
plus roughly 6 hours of your own work every week, not 3 hours total.

## Lab work converts differently

**2.5 hours of lab work = 3 credit hours.** Lab-heavy courses (common in the engineering departments
listed in [[Choosing a Department or School]]) accumulate credit faster per clock-hour than lecture
courses do, which is worth knowing when you're comparing two courses that both show "3 credits" on
the course list.

## The hard cap

**Maximum workload: no more than 66 credit hours in a single semester.** OGE's own materials translate
this into a real-world number: **roughly 55 clock hours of work every week** once lecture and the
2:1 outside-work ratio are both counted. That is close to a full-time job's hours, every week, for the
whole semester — plan the rest of your commitments (see [[Hostel and Mess Accommodation]] and
anything social) with that in mind, not against an idealized lighter courseload.

## How this connects to grading

Credits earned only count if you actually pass — see [[How Grading Works at IIT Madras]] for what the
S-to-U scale means, and [[Attendance Requirements]] for the attendance floor that can affect your
grade regardless of how well you did on the coursework itself.
