IIT Madras covers **632 acres (250 hectares)** of forest, with a lake, and people give directions by
landmark, not by address. These are the ones you will hear in your first week. They come from OGE's
[International Student Handbook 2026](https://ge.iitm.ac.in/assets/pdf/international-student-support-handbook.pdf?version=v2),
pages 1–2 and 12–15.

## The reference point

**Gajendra Circle (GC):** the main junction and the reference point for getting around campus. From
the main gate, Delhi Avenue and Bonn Avenue both lead here.

## Offices you will visit

- **Administration Block:** next to GC. It holds the Academic Section, Accounts, Security and other
  offices. You [[Collecting Your Student ID Card|collect your ID card]] from the Academic Courses
  Section, 4th floor. The **Security Section** is at the back of the ground floor.
- **Office of the Dean of Students (DoST):** next to the west end of the Administration Building.
  The **Wellness Centre** is on its first floor, and the **Up-Top Interaction Lounge (UTIL)** on its
  roof.
- **Office of Hostel Management (OHM / CCW):** handles hostel rooms and dining.
- **Institute Hospital:** on First Cross Road. See [[Health Care and Wellness Support]].

## Where you will study

- **Central Library:** a five-storey building near GC, with a **book bank** where you can borrow
  textbooks for a semester. **Ajanta**, its reading hall, is open **24/7**.
- **Class Room Complex (CRC):** on Alumni Avenue, halfway between the Administration Building and
  the Velachery Gate.
- **Raman and Ramanujan Blocks:** where many common classes are held.
- **Central Lecture Theatre (CLT):** air-conditioned, in the Humanities and Social Sciences block,
  just past the Central Library. Used for lectures, quizzes and cultural events.
- **New Academic Complex I and II (NAC-I, NAC-II):** NAC-I is next to Campus Cafe. NAC-II is next to
  Ocean Engineering, opposite Biotechnology-II.

## Where students gather

- **Students' Activity Centre (SAC):** music room, a large auditorium, and the sports facilities.
  Registration is held here.
- **Open Air Theatre (OAT):** between GC and the hostels. The Film Club screens a film **every
  Saturday**.
- **Quark:** a student activity space near Saraswathi hostel, with a restaurant on the ground floor.
- **Himalaya, Nilgiri and Vindhya dining facility:** next to Gurunath, with most of the messes. See
  [[Eating on Campus]].
- **Manohar C Watsa Stadium:** on Delhi Avenue.

For moving between these, see [[Getting Around Campus and Chennai]].
