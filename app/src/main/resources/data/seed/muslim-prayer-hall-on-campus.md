There is no separate mosque building on campus, but there is a **prayer hall for Muslim students**
where prayers are offered every day. IIT Madras lists it among the campus facilities, and the
student-run **MSA (Mu'min Students Association)** looks after it.

## Where it is

The two sources describe the spot differently, so check with MSA in your first week:

- **IIT Madras's own page:** behind the **Students Facilities Centre**, near **Himalaya Mess**.
- **MSA:** in front of the **IITM Gymkhana** building, opposite **Taramani Guest House**.

To get there by the campus shuttle, get off at the **Taramani Guest House** stop. See
[[Getting Around Campus and Chennai]] for the shuttle, and [[Campus Landmarks You Need to Know]]
for the places directions are given from.

## What to expect

- **Daily prayers** are held there regularly.
- **Friday prayer (Jumu'ah):** the sermon (khutbah) is given **in English**, so you will understand
  it even without Tamil, Urdu or Arabic. Start time: ___
- **Sisters** have a **separate entrance**.
- Wudu (ablution) area: ___
- Prayer times: MSA publishes them in the "Prayer Times" section of its site.
- The community also holds **Quran recitation** and other activities.

## Ramadan

Iftar, taraweeh and suhoor arrangements on campus and in the mess: ___ Ask MSA before Ramadan
starts; the mess timings are in [[Hostel and Mess Accommodation]].

## Who to ask

**MSA IIT Madras**: msa-iitm@smail.iitm.ac.in, website [msa-iitm.in](https://www.msa-iitm.in/).
They are students themselves and the quickest way to find the Friday time, the Ramadan plan and the
nearest mosques for Eid.

For mosques outside campus, see [[Mosques Near IIT Madras]].
