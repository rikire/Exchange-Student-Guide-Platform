Before you touch a single form, check that you actually clear the bar the Office of Global
Engagement (OGE) sets for the International Semester Exchange Programme. There are three
requirements, and all three apply:

1. **You're currently enrolled full-time** at an official partner institution of IIT Madras. Being
   admitted somewhere is not enough — you need to be an active, full-time student there when you
   apply.
2. **Your home institution has officially nominated you.** This is not something you can self-certify
   — the nomination comes from your home university to IIT Madras, and it's the trigger for
   [[Getting Your IIT Madras Invitation Letter]], the first real step of the application.
3. **You meet the academic and language proficiency requirements** set for the specific programme or
   course you want to take. These vary by department — see [[Choosing a Department or School]] and
   [[Course Selection Rules for UG and PG Students]] for what that means in practice once you're
   further along.

## What this does and doesn't tell you

This is the eligibility bar for the exchange programme as a whole. It does **not** cover per-course
prerequisites — those are checked later, when your department reviews your course selections on the
[[Selecting Courses on the SSP Portal|SSP portal]]. Clearing eligibility gets you into the process; it
doesn't guarantee every course you want is open to you.

## Needs checking with OGE before this goes in front of students

- What "official partner institution" means precisely — is there a published list, and where does a
  prospective student check it?
- Whether there's a minimum GPA or standardised language-test score, or whether "meets the
  requirements" is decided case-by-case by the receiving department.
