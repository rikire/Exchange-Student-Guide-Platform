OGE's own brochure lays the whole application out as three phases. If you only read one article
before starting, read this one — everything else in this guide is one of these three phases in
detail.

## Phase 1 — Invitation letters

Driven entirely by your home university's nomination. Once your home institution nominates you, IIT
Madras issues a student invitation letter, an incorporation letter, and an undertaking letter.
See [[Getting Your IIT Madras Invitation Letter]] for the exact flow, and
[[Exchange Programme Eligibility]] for what you need before this can even start.

## Phase 2 — Courses

You select and finalize your academic schedule through the SSP portal. This is where course
instructors can accept, reject, or suggest alternatives to what you've chosen — it's a real review,
not a formality. Full detail: [[Selecting Courses on the SSP Portal]].

## Phase 3 — Visa procedure

You use the SII (Study in India) portal to secure your travel documents. This phase depends on Phase
1 being finished — you cannot get your SII ID without the invitation letter IIT Madras uploads on
your behalf. See [[Registering on the SII Portal]] and [[Applying for Your Student Visa]].

## The order matters

These three phases are not independent tracks you can do in any order — Phase 3 literally cannot
start until IIT Madras's liaison has accepted your application and uploaded your invitation letter
inside the SII portal (that's step 3 of the Visa Flow in [[Registering on the SII Portal]]). If
something in your visa application feels stuck, the first thing to check is whether Phase 1 actually
finished on IIT Madras's side, not just on yours.

## After the visa: arriving

Getting the visa is not the end of the administrative process — [[Registering with FRRO]] and
[[Collecting Your Student ID Card]] both happen after you land, within tight windows. Don't treat the
visa as the finish line.
